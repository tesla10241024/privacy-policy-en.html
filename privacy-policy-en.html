HueLoom Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 24, 2026

HueLoom is designed to work without an account and without collecting personal data.

Data collection

HueLoom does not collect, transmit, sell, or use personal data for tracking, advertising, or analytics. The app does not operate a developer-controlled server.

Artwork and imported files

Your artwork, previews, imported images, text, and PSD content are processed on your device and stored in HueLoom's local app container. Imported files are accessed only after you select them with Apple's document picker.

Exports and sharing

When you choose to export or share content, Apple's system share sheet sends it to the destination you select. That destination may have its own privacy policy. HueLoom does not receive your shared content back from the destination.

Retention and deletion

Local artwork remains on your device until you delete the artwork or remove the app. Copies exported to other locations must be deleted from those locations separately.

Children

HueLoom does not knowingly collect personal information from children or adults.

Changes

If HueLoom's data practices change, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated.
